Privacy
Image files are read with the browser’s local file APIs. There is no upload endpoint, server inference, account, or image storage. The page has no analytics. AI runs on your GPU after downloading public model files from Hugging Face and GitHub; those hosts receive ordinary download requests, not your sentences or images. Browser caching uses storage on your device. Downloads can use about 2.4 GB of data. Loading and inference depend on GPU memory and browser support.

      
How to read Silex
Each glyph contains six binary sites, with weights 32, 16 / 8, 4 / 2, 1. Fixed head arities determine how pieces combine. Bound-variable distances make scope explicit. New concepts carry their sense definitions and argument roles inside a counted PAIR text-and-content package. The core codec decodes that package; this converter’s documented model supplies its lexical interpretation.
Automatic interpretation is experimental. Check the literal reading, especially pronouns, quantifiers, and tense. Exact text mode preserves wording without claiming to formalize its meaning. Decoding recovers the encoded meaning, not the source sentence’s original phrasing.
Upload a complete raster panel with its white margin. Integer-scaled and rotated panels are supported. Cropped panels, camera photos, captions, and blurred resizing may fail safely.
